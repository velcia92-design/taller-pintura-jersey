After a crash? Do these four things first.

It is stressful. This is the short version, whether you claim or pay yourself.

  1. Check everyone is safe

    Move to a safe place if you can. Call 999 if anyone is hurt or the road is blocked.

  2. Photograph everything

    Both cars, a close-up of the damage, the number plates and the position on the road.

  3. Swap details

    Name, phone, insurer and registration for every driver. Note any witnesses.

  4. Send us the photos

    We will tell you what needs doing and whether you can choose your own repairer.

General guidance, not legal or insurance advice. Always check your policy. If anyone is hurt, call 999 first.

Questions we get asked

Do I have to use my insurer’s repairer?

[Confirm for Jersey insurers.] In most cases you can choose your own repairer. Check your policy, or ask us and we will help.

How long will it take?

Small repairs can take a day or two. Larger jobs depend on parts and damage. We give you a date with your estimate.

Will the colour match?

We use your car’s paint code, test on a spray-out card and blend into neighbouring panels. Paint fades over time, so we match what is on your car now.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes, with no obligation. Photos give a good guide; we confirm in person.

Do you offer a courtesy car, collection or delivery?

[Confirm availability.] Ask when you book. If we cannot lend you a car, we will tell you before you leave yours with us.

Do you repair rust and underseal?

Yes. Jersey salt air is hard on sills, arches and the underside. We cut out rust properly and can renew underseal. [Confirm the exact treatment SVR offers.]

Do you work on hybrids and electric cars?

[Confirm with SVR.] We work on all makes and models. For high-voltage work, we would use a qualified specialist and tell you before we start.

Can you recalibrate driver-assistance systems after a repair?

[Confirm with SVR.] Cars with cameras and radar behind the windscreen or bumper often need recalibration after a repair. We can arrange it with a local specialist.

How do I pay?

[Confirm accepted payment methods.] We give you a written estimate first, and an invoice when the car is ready.

Do you scan the car before and after a repair?

[Confirm with SVR.] Modern cars have sensors and computers. If a scan is needed, we arrange it with a local specialist and include it in the estimate.

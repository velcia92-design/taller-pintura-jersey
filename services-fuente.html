Questions about repairs

How do I know which repair I need?

Send us photos or call in. We will tell you plainly, and if a smaller repair will do, we will say so.

Will the repaired panel match the rest of the car?

We match your paint code, test on a card and blend into the next panel. Paint fades over time, so we match what is on your car now.

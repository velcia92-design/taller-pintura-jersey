What a Car Check cannot tell you

  • Outstanding finance or past ownership. Ask the seller for the VRD1 and receipts.
  • Mileage history. Service records are your best guide.
  • Mechanical faults. For engine and gearbox, ask a mechanic too.

If the car was previously registered in the UK, an online history check on its old UK registration can add finance and write-off records.

2. Estimates and quotations

8. Privacy notice

This notice follows Article 12 of the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018. It explains what we do with your personal data.

Who controls your data

SVR – Sienna Vehicle Refinishing, [address], Jersey [JE_ ___]. Contact: [email], [phone]. We do not have a Data Protection Officer; the owners are responsible for data protection.

What we collect and where it comes from

Everything comes from you: your name, phone number, email, registration, car make and model, a description of the damage, photos you choose to send, and the messages you send us. If you use our online booking, we also keep the day and time you pick. We do not buy data or collect it from third parties.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and give an estimate — steps taken at your request before a contract (Article 8(2)(b)).
  • To carry out the repair and keep you updated — performance of our contract with you (Article 8(2)(b)).
  • To keep invoices and records for tax and accounting — our legal obligation (Article 8(2)(c)).
  • To handle a complaint or a guarantee claim — our legitimate interests in putting things right (Article 8(2)(f)).
  • To send you photos and progress updates by WhatsApp — your consent or the contract, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Marketing — we do not send marketing without your consent, and we do not sell your data.

Do you have to give it?

We need your name and a way to contact you to answer you, and your registration and photos to estimate and carry out the work. Without them we cannot help. Everything else is optional; you can leave a field empty.

Who sees it

  • Our own staff and owners, who need it to do the work.
  • Your insurer, only if you ask us to deal with them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) when you choose to contact us that way. WhatsApp handles the message under its own privacy policy.
  • Our accountant and any IT support we use, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

Sending data outside Jersey

9. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and it loads nothing from third parties. Fonts, images and scripts come from this site.

It saves one small preference in your browser, svr-lang, so the site remembers your language. It stays on your device, never reaches us, and you can delete it by clearing site data in your browser settings. The site will simply start in English again.

Because there is nothing to track, there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.
